Dr. Qiusheng Wu is an Associate Professor and the Director of Graduate Studies
in the Department of Geography and Sustainability at the University of
Tennessee, Knoxville. He also serves as an Amazon Scholar.

His research focuses on geospatial data science and open-source software
development, with an emphasis on leveraging big geospatial data and cloud
computing to study environmental change, particularly surface water and wetland
inundation dynamics.

He is the creator of several widely used open-source Python packages, including
[geemap](https://geemap.org), [leafmap](https://leafmap.org),
[segment-geospatial](https://samgeo.gishub.org), and
[geoai](https://opengeoai.org), which support advanced geospatial analysis and
interactive visualization. Several of them are used directly in this course. His
open-source work is available through
[Open Geospatial Solutions](https://github.com/opengeos) on GitHub.

## Coming to Office Hours

You do not need a reason to come, though bringing broken code is a perfectly
good one. Office hours are the fastest way to get unstuck, and they are almost
always empty in the first few weeks, which is exactly when a half hour of help
is worth the most.

If the scheduled times do not work with your class schedule, email me and we
will find another slot.

## Getting Help Between Meetings

Technical questions belong on the
[GitHub Discussion board](https://github.com/giswqs/gispro-fall26/discussions)
rather than in my inbox, so that the whole class benefits from the answer. I
reply within one business day. Anything personal or private should come to me by
email instead.
