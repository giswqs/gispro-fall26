## How to Use the Videos

The videos supplement the book and the in-class sessions rather than replacing
them. They are most useful for:

- Watching code get written and executed instead of only reading it
- Hearing a second explanation of a concept that did not land the first time
- Seeing how to approach a problem and debug it step by step

Read the assigned chapter first, then watch the matching video, then work
through the lab. Pause the video and type the code yourself as you follow along.
